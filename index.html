Day1

Arrival in Sanur

🗺️ Maps: Add Map Link
Monday, 23 June · Arrive noon
🏨 Puri Santrian Beach Resort & Spa, Sanur
🚗
Transport Airport → Sanur: Pre-book a private cab or use Bluebird taxi (metered, most reliable). ~30–40 min, ~IDR 150–200k. Avoid touts inside arrivals. All Sanur activities are walkable along the 5 km beach boardwalk. Rent bicycles (IDR 50k/day) from your hotel for easy exploration.
📍 Arrival Note Check in, freshen up. Sanur is the perfect relaxed entry into Bali — calm beach, no surf crowds, great food. The famous 5 km beachfront boardwalk links everything.
☀️ Afternoon Activities (2 – 5:30 PM)
  • ⠿🚶Sanur Beachfront Boardwalk5 km paved path lined with colourful jukung boats — Bali's most photogenic fishing boats. Walk north toward Sunrise Beach area.📸 Photo: boats against Agung volcano backdrop
  • ⠿🛕Pura Blanjong TempleOldest dated inscription in Bali (914 AD). Small, serene, very local — just 10 min walk inland from beach.
  • ⠿🪁Sindhu Beach MarketA small afternoon market near Jl. Danau Tamblingan selling local snacks, fresh coconut, batik wraps. Great intro to local life.📸 Photo: colourful offerings & daily life
  • ⠿🌅Sanur Sunrise Beach ViewpointDespite the name, beautiful at any time. Clear views of Nusa Penida and Mt. Agung. Perfect golden hour shot.📸 Photo: golden hour Agung reflection
🍽️ Lunch (1 – 2 PM)
🥇 Warung Mak Beng
Bali's most legendary warung. Only one menu — crispy fried fish, spicy fish soup, sambal & rice. IDR 55k/person. Queue expected; arrive by 12:30 or after 2 PM. Jl. Hang Tuah No.45
🔄 Alternative: Moreno Beach Bali
Italian-Indonesian fusion right on the sand. Great for arriving weary travellers who want to ease in gently. Pasta, fresh barramundi. Jl. Segara Ayu No.42
🌙 Dinner – Food Street Exploration
🦞 Warung Be Sanur
Offshoot of Mak Beng, slightly different spice. Order the grilled fish, chicken satay & Balinese black rice pudding for dessert.
🌃 Jl. Danau Tamblingan Night Walk
Sanur's main restaurant strip. Walk this after 7 PM for everything from BBQ corn stalls to fresh fruit juice carts. Try Es Campur (shaved ice dessert) from street vendors.
💡 Food Explorer Tips for Sanur Try Nasi Campur (mixed rice plate), Sate Lilit (minced fish satay on lemongrass skewers — Bali specialty), Lawar (minced meat & coconut salad), and finish with a Bintang beer or young coconut on the beach at sunset.
Day2

Nusa Lembongan Island

🗺️ Maps: Add Map Link
Tuesday, 24 June · Full Day
🏨 Return to Puri Santrian, Sanur
⛵
Boat from Sanur Harbour Fast boat departs from Sanur Harbour (Jl. Hang Tuah near Mak Beng). Book operators like Rocky Fast Cruise or Scoot Fast Cruises (IDR 250–350k one way). Depart 8–9 AM, return by 4–5 PM. Crossing ~35 min. Book 1 day ahead. On the island, hire a local scooter driver for IDR 150–200k/day to cover all spots.
🌅 Morning (9:30 AM – 12:30 PM)
  • ⠿🌊Devil's TearDramatic cliff where waves crash violently into a natural channel, spraying 15 m into the air. Sunrise or early morning best. Most iconic spot on Lembongan.📸 Photo: wave spray silhouette shot
  • ⠿🌿Mangrove ForestGlide through emerald mangrove tunnels by canoe or small boat. Serene, otherworldly green light.📸 Photo: light through mangrove canopy
  • ⠿🐠Snorkelling – Crystal Bay / Mangrove BayBrilliant coral with clownfish, turtles & if lucky — Manta Rays. Rent snorkel gear from any beach shack (IDR 50k). For Mantas, go to Manta Point via boat tour.
🌤️ Afternoon (2 – 5 PM)
  • ⠿🌉Yellow Bridge – Nusa CeninganThe iconic suspension bridge connecting Lembongan to Ceningan island. Walk or scooter across for the view.📸 Photo: bridge with island backdrop
  • ⠿💙Blue Lagoon, CeninganBrilliant turquoise cove with cliff jump platforms. Cafe above for drinks with the view (Klyf Club has a stunning terrace).📸 Photo: aerial-style shot from cliff
  • ⠿🏄Dream Beach / Mushroom BeachSwim in calm white-sand bays. Rent a sun lounger and decompress before the boat back.
🍽️ Lunch on Lembongan
🥥 Sandy Bay Beach Club
Beachfront daybeds, pool, fresh seafood. Grilled fish platters, nasi goreng & coconut mojitos. Mid-range.
🐟 The Deck Café
Local warung near main village. Grilled fish of the day, Indonesian sides. Very affordable.
🌙 Dinner – Back in Sanur
🎵 Three Monkeys Café
Chilled garden setting, live music evenings, great grilled meats and Indonesian fare. Very popular with families.
🍢 Pregina Warung
Beloved local spot for Balinese duck (bebek betutu) and babi guling (suckling pig). Must try authentic Balinese flavours. Book ahead.
Day3

East Bali – Royal Water Palaces & Sacred Peaks

🗺️ Maps: Add Map Link
Wednesday, 25 June · Relaxed Start (depart 8:30 AM)
🏨 Return to Puri Santrian, Sanur
🚗
Hired Private Car & Driver (full day) Hire from hotel or Klook/GetYourGuide. Full day east Bali circuit: IDR 600–800k. Route: Sanur → Lempuyang (2.5 hrs) → Tirta Gangga (20 min from Lempuyang) → Besakih (1 hr from Tirta Gangga) → optional Sidemen Valley → back to Sanur. ~200 km round trip. Driver handles parking; you focus on photography.
⏰ Timing Tip for Lempuyang Arrive by 9–9:30 AM. The queue for the Gate of Heaven photo grows to 2–3 hours by midday. Early arrivals wait ~30–45 min. Wear modest dress (sarong provided). No mirror at the base — it's a phone held under the gate for the reflection shot.
🌅 Morning – East Bali Temples
  • ⠿🚪Lempuyang Temple – Gates of Heaven, testing editingBali's most photographed temple gate with Mt. Agung framed perfectly between split gates. Entry ~IDR 100k. The "floating" reflection is done with a mirror/water held under the gate by attendants.📸 #1 Most iconic Bali photo
  • ⠿💦Tirta Gangga Water PalaceFormer royal water palace with elegant stepped pools, giant koi fish, stone stepping paths, and lush gardens against a highland backdrop. Entry IDR 90k. You can swim in the bathing pools.📸 Stepping stones, lotus pools, Agung backdrop
☀️ Afternoon – Mother Temple
  • ⠿🏔️Besakih – Mother Temple of BaliThe holiest and largest temple complex on the slopes of Mt. Agung. Over 80 shrines. Entry IDR 150k. Hire an official guide inside (not the touts at entrance). Dress: full sarong + sash.📸 Multi-tiered merus (pagodas) against Agung
  • ⠿🌾Sidemen Valley (drive-through)On the return route — one of Bali's most beautiful river valleys with lush terraced rice fields. Ask driver to stop at Pura Kehen viewpoint for photos.📸 Valley panorama, traditional weaving villages
🍽️ Lunch near Tirta Gangga
🍛 Warung Stana Angga
Right inside Tirta Gangga complex. Simple warung with views over the pools. Nasi campur, grilled fish, fresh coconut. Authentic & affordable.
🌿 Café Saraswati Sidemen
On the Sidemen road with rice terrace views. Local Balinese cuisine, cold beer, shaded terrace. Great rest stop.
🌙 Dinner – Sanur Farewell
🌊 Chez Gado Gado / Massimo
Massimo on Danau Tamblingan: beloved Italian-Balinese with excellent gelato. Or try Char Ming for French Balinese fusion — great grilled seafood.
🥓 Bebek Bengil (Dirty Duck)
Iconic crispy duck restaurant. Famous throughout Bali. Book ahead. Also has an Ubud branch.
Day4

Sanur → Ubud via Sightseeing

🗺️ Maps: Add Map Link
Thursday, 26 June · Check-in Ubud by noon
🏨 Villa Ubud Sunshine, Jalan Bisma Ubud
🚗
Hired Driver: Sanur → Ubud (sightseeing route) Route via Celuk (gold/silver village) → Mas (wood carving village) → Tegalalang → Ubud. ~2.5 hrs with stops. IDR 400–500k for the half-day. Arrive Ubud ~11:30 AM. After check-in, explore Ubud on foot — Jalan Bisma is walkable to the palace and market.
🌅 En Route to Ubud (8:30 – 11:30 AM)
  • ⠿🥈Celuk – Silver & Gold VillageBali's jewellery heartland. Watch master silversmiths work by hand. Excellent place to buy authentic Balinese silver jewellery. Start at smaller workshops on side streets for better prices.
  • ⠿🪵Mas – Wood Carving VillageRoadside galleries of intricate handmade Garuda statues, masks, panels. Great for quality gifts and art.
  • ⠿🌾Tegalalang Rice TerracesUNESCO-listed emerald terraced paddies plunging into a gorge. Swing & zip-line activities available. Arrive 9 AM for best light and fewer people.📸 Iconic Bali rice terrace, gorge shots
☀️ Ubud Afternoon (2 – 5 PM)
  • ⠿🏛️Puri Saren Agung – Ubud PalaceStill-occupied royal palace at the heart of Ubud. Free entry. Beautiful split gates (candi bentar), carved thrones, frangipani-filled courtyards. Evening Kecak dance shows here.📸 Golden gates, courtyard carvings
  • ⠿🛍️Ubud Art Market (Pasar Seni)Right opposite the palace. Two floors of batik, carved wooden masks, rattan bags, silver jewellery, paintings. Bargain hard — start at 30–40% of asking price.
  • ⠿🚶Jalan Bisma WalkYour hotel's street is one of Ubud's most beautiful — a quiet road with rice terrace views and excellent boutique restaurants. Great evening stroll.📸 Gorge views along Bisma ridge
🍽️ Lunch in Ubud
🏆 This Is Bali – Balinese Food & Desserts
4.9 ★ with 16,000+ reviews. Build-your-own Balinese rice bowl or choose from set bowls. Jackfruit, duck, rendang options. Jl. Goutama No.2. Excellent value, stunning presentation. A must.
🌙 Dinner + Kecak Dance
🔥 Kecak Fire Dance at Ubud Palace
7:30 PM. Tickets IDR 100–150k. The chanting, fire, and Ramayana story-telling under flaming torches — unmissable cultural experience. Book tickets at the gate by 7 PM.
🍝 Donna Restaurant (Post-Show)
Top-rated Ubud restaurant. Creative menu — gnocchi, lamb ragu, octopus. Modern ambiance with cocktails. Jl. Monkey Forest. Book ahead.
Day5

Ubud Deep Dive – Temples, Walks & Markets

🗺️ Maps: Add Map Link
Friday, 27 June · Full Day in Ubud
🏨 Villa Ubud Sunshine, Jalan Bisma
🚶
Walking Day + 1 Hired Car for Afternoon Morning is all walkable from your Bisma villa. Campuhan Ridge Walk starts 10 min walk from the villa. Afternoon: hire a driver (IDR 200–300k half day) for Tanah Lot sunset. Or take Grab to specific spots.
🌅 Morning (6:30 – 11:30 AM)
  • ⠿🌄Campuhan Ridge WalkDepart at 6:30–7 AM from Pura Gunung Lebah (start of the ridge). 3 km easy walk along a green ridge with panoramic valley views. End at Karsa Kafe for breakfast with views.📸 Best golden morning light on rice hills
  • ⠿🐒Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary750+ Balinese long-tailed macaques roam free among 3 ancient temples. Atmospheric moss-covered paths. Entry IDR 120k. Keep bags zipped, no food visible, remove glasses (monkeys grab them!).📸 Ancient moss-covered temple, monkey portraits
  • ⠿🎨Ubud Museum Puri LukisanFinest collection of traditional Balinese paintings. Beautiful garden complex. Entry IDR 100k. Great context for Bali's art tradition.
☀️ Afternoon – Tanah Lot Sunset
  • ⠿🛕Taman Ayun Royal TempleMengwi kingdom's royal temple, moated and serene. Stunning multi-tiered merus reflected in surrounding water. Entry IDR 50k. Quieter than other temples.📸 Moat reflections of tiered pagodas
  • ⠿🌊Tanah Lot Temple – SunsetBali's most famous sea temple perched on a coastal rock. Arrive 4:30–5 PM for golden light then sunset. Entry IDR 75k. Evening Kecak performance here too (7 PM, IDR 150k — optional second show).📸 #2 Most iconic Bali shot — temple in ocean
🍽️ Lunch
☕ Karsa Kafe (post-ridge walk)
Perched on the ridge at walk's end. Full breakfast, fresh juices, nasi goreng. Rice terrace views. Open from 8 AM.
🍜 Warung Teges
Classic Balinese warung, extremely popular with locals. Babi guling (suckling pig) and duck. Open lunch only. Queue by 11:30 AM.
🌙 Dinner
🌿 Bridges Bali
Restaurant literally built over the Campuhan river gorge. Dramatic setting, candlelit, international & Balinese menu. Excellent for a celebratory family dinner. Book ahead.
🍛 Locavore
Award-winning fine dining using only Balinese local ingredients. Tasting menu. Special occasion dinner. Book 2–3 weeks ahead.
Day6

Ubud → Nusa Dua via UNESCO Terraces

🗺️ Maps: Add Map Link
Saturday, 28 June · Check-in Nusa Dua evening
🏨 The Ayodya Palace, Jl. Pantai Mengiat, Nusa Dua
🚗
Hired Car: Ubud → Jatiluwih → Nusa Dua Full day hire: IDR 700–900k. Route: Ubud → Jatiluwih Rice Terraces (1.5 hr) → Tanah Lot area for lunch → Uluwatu Temple (2 hr drive south) → Jimbaran Bay dinner → Nusa Dua hotel check-in. Note: Grab/Gojek cannot enter Jatiluwih; private car essential.
🌅 Morning – UNESCO Terraces
  • ⠿🌾Jatiluwih Rice TerracesUNESCO World Heritage Site. Far grander and less commercial than Tegalalang. Walk the medium trail (2.5 km, 1.5 hrs). Entry IDR 150k. Café midway with sweeping terrace views. Far more authentic than Tegalalang.📸 Panoramic green staircase terraces
  • ⠿⛺Pura Luhur BatukaruAncient temple in the mist-covered forest on Batukaru volcano. Rarely visited by tourists — deeply atmospheric and spiritual. On the road to Jatiluwih.📸 Temple in jungle mist
🌤️ Afternoon – Cliff Temples
  • ⠿🦅Uluwatu Temple & Cliff WalkDramatic 70 m cliffs above the Indian Ocean. Entry IDR 50k. The cliff walking trail spans 1 km with ocean panoramas. IMPORTANT: store sunglasses in bag — resident monkeys are notorious thieves!📸 Temple on cliff edge, white ocean crash below
  • ⠿🔥Kecak Fire Dance at UluwatuThe most dramatic version — 6 PM show at the clifftop amphitheatre with the sun setting behind. 100+ chanters, fire dancing. IDR 150k. Arrive 5:30 PM for seats.📸 Silhouette fire dancer against sunset ocean
🍽️ Lunch en route
🌾 Jatiluwih Restaurant
Inside the rice terrace complex. Panoramic views of the terraces while you eat. Balinese set meals with smoked duck, tempe, local vegetables. Unforgettable setting.
🦞 Dinner – Jimbaran Bay Seafood
🌊 Jimbaran Bay Seafood Cafes
One of the great Bali dining experiences. Dozens of open-air seafood BBQ restaurants right on the sand. Tables lit by lanterns at water's edge. Choose your fish/lobster/prawns by weight, watch them grill it. Try: Menega Café, Lia Café, or Jimbaran Beach Cafe. IDR 200–400k/person.
After dinner, 30 min drive to Nusa Dua. Check in to Ayodya Palace.
Day7

Nusa Dua – Beach, Culture & GWK

🗺️ Maps: Add Map Link
Sunday, 29 June · Full Day Nusa Dua
🏨 The Ayodya Palace, Nusa Dua
🚗
In-Resort + Local Taxi Nusa Dua is a self-contained resort zone — walkable within the complex. For GWK and Seminyak, use Grab/Gojek (~IDR 50–80k one way). The resort has bikes available. Water sports are bookable at your beach concierge.
🌅 Morning – Beach & Water Sports
  • ⠿🏊Nusa Dua BeachCalm, clear, protected lagoon beach — safest swimming in Bali. Perfect for the family. No aggressive vendors. Book watersports: jet ski, parasailing, banana boat, or snorkelling from your hotel concierge.
  • ⠿🌊Water Blow, Nusa DuaNatural rock formation where waves create massive water explosions similar to Devil's Tear. 10 min walk from resort area.📸 Dramatic water explosion shots
  • ⠿🛍️Bali Collection MallThe upscale open-air shopping centre within the Nusa Dua zone. Good for souvenirs, clothing, last-minute gifts, and air-conditioned cool.
☀️ Afternoon – GWK & Seminyak
  • ⠿🦅Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK) Cultural ParkThe 121 m tall Garuda Wisnu statue — tallest in the world. Stunning views across southern Bali. Cultural performances, traditional dance shows, photography zones. Entry IDR 150k adults. 2–3 hours. Don't miss the guided climb into the statue.📸 Massive statue against Bali skyline
  • ⠿🏪Seminyak – Late Afternoon BrowseBali's hippest neighbourhood: Jl. Kayu Aya (Eat Street), boutique stores, designer Bali home goods. If the family wants nightlife/cocktails — rooftop bars along Jl. Petitenget.
🍽️ Lunch at Nusa Dua
🍛 Nusa Indonesian Gastronomy
Inside Bali Collection. Exceptional presentation of Indonesian island cuisines — try the Makassar coto soup, West Sumatra rendang, Javanese gudeg alongside Balinese dishes.
🌊 The Patio at Ayodya
Your hotel's beachfront restaurant. Splurge on a beach lunch — grilled lobster or fresh fish with resort views.
🌙 Dinner – Seminyak
🔥 Ku De Ta / Merah Putih
Merah Putih on Jl. Petitenget: breathtaking colonial warehouse interior with 14 m ceilings, extensive menu of modern Indonesian cuisine. Worth every rupiah. Book ahead.
🍣 Sarong Restaurant
Award-winning pan-Asian fine dining. Chef Will Meyrick's signature menu. One of Bali's consistently best restaurants.
Day8

Last Full Day – Spa, Surf & Sunset

🗺️ Maps: Add Map Link
Monday, 30 June · Last Day · Checkout 1 Jul 7 AM
🏨 The Ayodya Palace, Nusa Dua
🚗
Grab/Local Taxi for Day Trips Kuta is 20 min from Nusa Dua by Grab. Seminyak 30 min. Pack your bags the night before — checkout is 7 AM on 1 July. Leave bags with concierge the evening before and enjoy a relaxed final day.
🧳 Packing Tip for Departure Your flight is likely mid-morning. Nusa Dua to airport is only 15 min. Enjoy a full final day — leave hotel bags at 6:45 AM and reach airport easily. Pack the night before.
🌅 Morning – Spa or Surf
  • ⠿💆Ayodya SpaBook the Balinese 4-hands massage or traditional boreh body scrub (turmeric & spice). One of the finest resort spas in South Bali. Book the previous evening.
  • ⠿🏄Surf Lesson at Kuta BeachKuta is the best beginner surf beach in Bali. Lessons IDR 200–350k including board (1.5 hr). Great activity for the 22 and 18-year-olds. Multiple surf schools on the beach.
  • ⠿🪸Benoa Marine Park SnorkelShort 20 min boat from Nusa Dua to Benoa Bay — decent snorkelling, sea turtles at turtle conservation island.
☀️ Afternoon – Kuta & Sunset
  • ⠿🏖️Kuta Beach & Legian WalkBali's legendary surfer beach. Walk the strip, watch the waves, try fresh coconut. Very different vibe from Sanur — lively, commercial, but essential Bali.📸 Kuta surf crowd, long beach sunset
  • ⠿🌅Petitenget Beach SunsetSuperior to Kuta for sunsets — less crowded, beautiful beach temple (Pura Petitenget), lined with fashionable beach clubs. Sundowner here is the perfect Bali finale.📸 Final sunset — temple silhouette at dusk
  • ⠿🛍️Kuta / Legian Last-Minute ShoppingKuta Square, Legian market for final souvenirs. Batik shirts, sarongs, Bali coffee, Luwak civet coffee packs, silver jewellery.
🍽️ Lunch
🥥 Naughty Nuri's Warung
Famous for melt-off-the-bone BBQ pork ribs. Originally an Ubud institution — now also in Seminyak. Casual, no reservations, great for a relaxed last lunch.
🌮 Motel Mexicola
Colourful, festive Mexican-Balinese restaurant in Seminyak. Great for the young adults in the family — tacos, quesadillas, mezcal, DJ music.
🌙 Farewell Dinner
🌊 Sundara Beach Club, Four Seasons Jimbaran
Elegant sunset dinner on the beach. Fresh grilled seafood, whole lobster, Balinese suckling pig. The best send-off dinner in South Bali. Reserve in advance.
🏰 Back at Ayodya
For a relaxed early night before 7 AM checkout, your hotel's restaurant does an excellent Balinese set dinner. Earlier bedtime = better departure energy.
